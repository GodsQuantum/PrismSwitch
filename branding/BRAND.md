# PrismSwitch — identity guide

**Name:** PrismSwitch
**Tagline:** Two inputs. One screen.
**Personality:** calm, precise, dependable, understated. A native Linux utility, not a cloud service.

## Visual language

Two colored signal paths merge into one screen through a diamond-shaped routing mark. Teal signifies the selected input, violet a second source, foam the common output. No Apple logo or implied endorsement. The icon contains no text. Its canonical source is SVG; retain the high-contrast silhouette and avoid thin strokes at small sizes.
