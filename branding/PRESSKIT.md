# PrismSwitch — press kit

**Product:** PrismSwitch
**Tagline:** Two inputs. One screen.
**Pitch:** Give a compatible vintage iMac a second life as a DisplayPort monitor under Linux.

**Description:** Independent fork of floe/smc_util with a local Python controller, configurable keyboard shortcuts, reversible power profiles, a terminal setup wizard and hardware-aware safeguards. No account, network service or telemetry.

**Brand assets:** logo-mark.svg (128 square), logo-mono.svg (monochrome), logo.svg (wordmark), banner.svg (1200 × 400), social-preview.svg (1200 × 630). Canonical editable SVGs; do not distort.

**Status:** 0.1.0 preview. The older deployment has been used successfully on one iMac11,1. The new controller has automated tests and a live read-only SMC test; its full hardware switching and audible sound require further qualification.
